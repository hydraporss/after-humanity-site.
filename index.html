Every deliverable is a controlled package

Studio X treats a deliverable as more than a file. It carries an ID, a version, evidence and an approval state, and each completion state is kept separate.

GeneratedVerifiedAcceptedReleasedProduction-ready

DRAFT → REVIEW → APPROVED → SUPERSEDED → RETIRED

What a deliverable records

  • Identity: a stable ID and version, such as INTEL-RPT-2025-001 v1.0, not just a filename.
  • Evidence: claims link back to their sources, and the app labels a finding as verified, interpreted or assumed.
  • Integrity: each artifact carries a SHA-256 checksum.
  • Authority: reviewer and approver stay blank until a person actually reviews and approves.

What it won’t do for you

  • Call work done on a build alone. Tests and checks against acceptance criteria come first.
  • Approve its own output. AI agents propose and produce evidence; people approve.
  • Overwrite a known-good version. Candidate, accepted and release copies stay separate.
  • Treat READY as released. Release is its own decision.
